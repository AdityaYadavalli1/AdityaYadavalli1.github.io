I am a first-year Ph.D. student in the LeM🍋N Lab at UC San Diego, where I am advised by [Prof. Alex Warstadt](http://alexwarstadt.github.io). Here, I work on using spoken language models (SLMs) to study how information is conveyed through lexical (textual) and non-lexical (speech) streams of human communication.

Previously, I was a Speech/NLP Engineer at [Karya](https://www.karya.in/), where I worked on multilingual evaluation, endangered language resources, crowdsourced speech datasets, and assistive tools for crowdsource workers. I have also volunteered with [Masakhane](https://www.masakhane.io), studying speech and language models for African languages and accents.

From 2021-2023, I was a visiting researcher at Case Western Reserve University, where I worked with [Prof. Vera Tobin](https://veratobin.org) on modeling second language acquisition using child-directed speech. I completed my B.Tech. (Hons) in Computer Science and M.S. by Research in Computational Linguistics at [IIIT Hyderabad](https://www.iiit.ac.in), advised by [Prof. Anil Kumar Vuppala](https://www.iiit.ac.in/people/faculty/anilvuppala/). My M.S. thesis explored how closely related languages can improve speech recognition for low-resource languages.
